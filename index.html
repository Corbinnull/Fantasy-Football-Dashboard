Fantasy HQ

One dashboard for every league — prioritize lineups, understand exposure, and see which players matter most to your week.

Heads up: Sleeper's API is read-only, so this tool can't submit lineup changes for you — there's no official way for any third-party app to do that. It builds your plan and gives you a direct link into each league; the final tap happens in the Sleeper app.
